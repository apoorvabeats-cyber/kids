# FunLearn

A kids learning simulator. Tap a card to hear it spoken aloud.

**Live the same experience here:** open `index.html` in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages on this repository.

## What is inside

- English alphabets A–Z with a word for each letter
- Hindi varnmala (swar and vyanjan)
- Numbers 1–100
- Animals, birds, and everyday sounds
- Nursery rhymes and short English sentences
- Days of the week and months of the year
- Rashtra Gaan and Rashtra Geet
- Freedom fighters
- Play-all, stop, and a listen-and-pick quiz
- Stars for cards you have already heard (saved in this browser)

## How to use

1. Open [index.html](index.html).
2. Pick a topic.
3. Tap a card. Allow sound if the browser asks.
4. Try **Quiz** in alphabet, number, animal, and similar topics.

Hindi speech needs a Hindi voice on the device. Chrome, Edge, and Safari all work.

## Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. Progress stays in local storage on the device.
